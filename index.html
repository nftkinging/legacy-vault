Before you seal anything

Is my letter really private?

The letter itself is encrypted client-side before it's stored on-chain — nobody can read it by browsing the explorer. But who you've named as a beneficiary, and how much BOT is locked, are not private: both are public on-chain by design, the same way any transaction is. Don't rely on this for anonymity about the relationship itself, only for the contents of the letter.

Is my money ever trapped?

No — you can withdraw at any time while you're active. The one exception: a small minimum stays locked in a live vault to keep it real (deters spam vaults with no funds behind them). To reclaim that last portion, close the vault entirely rather than withdrawing it down to zero.

What if I don't check in in time?

Nothing happens instantly. Once your chosen silence interval passes, your named beneficiary becomes able to claim the vault and read the letter — but you can still check in or withdraw right up until the moment they do.
