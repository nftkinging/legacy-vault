Legacy Vault

You may have been left something

Legacy Vault lets someone lock funds and a private letter for you, to be handed over only if they go quiet for a long time — months or years, whatever they chose. If you've been named, you don't have to do anything right now to eventually receive it.

There's one optional step, though, that makes the letter itself safer to read.

Why register

Right now, if someone names you, they also have to hand you a secret passphrase separately — in person, by message, however they can. If that passphrase never reaches you, the letter can never be opened, even though any funds left for you are still yours to claim.

Registering removes that risk entirely. It publishes a key that only you hold, so anyone who names you afterward can lock their letter to you directly — no passphrase for anyone to lose, forget, or fail to deliver in time.

Before you start

  • You'll need a crypto wallet — an app like MetaMask that holds a small amount of BOT to cover the network fee. If you don't have one yet, the connect button above will offer to set one up.
  • This is one small transaction on BOT Chain. It costs a tiny network fee, paid from your own wallet — Legacy Vault doesn't charge anything.
  • At the end, you'll be shown a backup file. Save it somewhere you won't lose it — a password manager, a drive, printed and filed away. It's the only way to read a letter if you ever open one from a different device.

Common questions

Is this safe? Is this a scam?

Registering only publishes a key — it never asks for money, seed phrases, or passwords from you, and it never sends anything anywhere. The one transaction you approve is visible in your wallet before you confirm it, and on the public explorer afterward.

Do I need to do this right now?

No. Registering only matters before someone encrypts a letter to you — once that's happened, registering later triggers a prompt for them to re-encrypt it to your new key, but there's no deadline on your end.

What if I don't have a crypto wallet?

The connect button at the top of this page will offer to help you set one up if it doesn't find one already. It only takes a couple of minutes.

What does this cost?

A small BOT Chain network fee — typically a fraction of a cent's worth of computation, paid to the network, not to Legacy Vault. You'll see the exact amount in your wallet before confirming.
